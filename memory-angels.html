Memory Angels

Sculpture · Photo · Photoshop

This was a collaborative project with Madeline Drumheller. She started by making a wire sculpture which vaguely resembled a face.

I turned the image into a solid silhouette of the sculpture, and began duplicating, flipping, and mirroring the image onto itself to create interesting patterns.

And then began combining these patterns with photographs taken by Madeline.

And then created a grid of 12 photos to be printed as a zine.

I have to dig the zine out of box so I can upload a finished photo of it.
